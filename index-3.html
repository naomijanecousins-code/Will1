Will Builder

LLB Year 3 — Student Drafting Exercise

Student version
Task: Create your own fictional will. Enter only the fictional testator’s name, specific gifts and the residue of the estate.
Teaching draft only. This activity is for classroom use and is not a document for execution.

1. Testator

Use a fictional name.

2. Specific gifts

Record each gift clearly enough to identify the property and intended beneficiary.

Consider whether the gift and beneficiary are identified precisely enough for a draft will.

3. Residue of the estate

Divide the residue between one or more fictional beneficiaries. Shares must total 100%.

4. Review and submit

Review the legal drafting: check that the testator is named, gifts are sufficiently clear, and the residue accounts for the whole estate.
